## About

The Gryphon II is LURA’s 2nd flagship rocket. With it we are trying to break the UK amateur rocketry altitude
record from British soil. The vehicle is designed for Mach 2.3 and an apogee of 13 km. I am the Project Lead,
manging the timelines and finances while working on several facets of the design. My notable contributions
are to the Avionics Bay oversight and design of the “universal coupler” mechanism which allows us to
assemble the rocket in chunks, each coupling requiring only one bolt whilst providing a robust mechanical
and electrical interface.
