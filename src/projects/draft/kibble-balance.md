## About

In 2019 the SI definition of a kilogram switched from the mass of a platinum-iridium cylinder to being in terms
of Planck’s constant. Kibble balances (formerly “Watt balances”) are used to make absolute mass
measurements under this new regime. This idea came from Bryan Kibble’s work at NPL in the 1970s. During
my time at NPL, I supported the Kibble Balance team with activities including bespoke voltmeter testing,
circuit board design, writing Python and Bash scripts and administering the 10+ industrial computers I built for
instrument control.
