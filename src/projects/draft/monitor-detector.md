## About
Acting on behalf of CNES, Airbus commissioned NPL to produce STAR-CC-OGSE to provide traceable
optical calibration for the MicroCarb satellite. I was primarily responsible for delivering the Monitor Detector
which sampled the instrument’s light source allowing it to be traceably calibrated. The detector had to
operate in vacuum, manage its thermals and capture readings from Si and InGaAs photodiodes and a
commercial spectrometer. I designed the mechanics for holding and aligning the optics, the electronics for
reading off the photodiodes and the electrical cabinet which housed all the supporting equipment.
