## About

As a team of six NPL apprentices, we built “NoProbLemo” to compete in the BBC’s Robot Wars. The show
was unfortunately cancelled before we could compete however, we saw the project though and learnt a
great deal. I was responsible for much of the mechanical design and drafting. The robot was ~110kg and
span a 30kg drum above 6,000 RPM. Key takeaways were the importance of delegation and critical design
review before starting manufacture.
