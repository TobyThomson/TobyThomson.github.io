## About

Some colleagues and I attempted to reclaim NPL’s world record for the largest water rocket with a 5.5m tall
structure built from ~700 2L water bottles. Unfortunately, a sticky hold-down release mechanism scuppered
the record attempt. The experience instilled in me a great appreciation for verification testing and
operational procedures.
