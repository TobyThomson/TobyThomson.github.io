Zola was a quick project with a four-person team to figure out how to go about building a liquid oxygen pump. We tested it at Race to Space 2024/25 and were able to generate a couple of bar pressure delta before the pressure boundary failed. The learnings fed into the devlopment of Meela, our subsequent, more serious, attempt.
